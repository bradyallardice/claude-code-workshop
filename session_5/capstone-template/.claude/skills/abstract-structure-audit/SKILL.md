# Abstract Structure Audit

Check whether the abstract contains:

1. The research question.
2. The design and data: conjoint experiment, Magistro et al. 2026, US and Canada YouGov sample.
3. The replication or extension finding.
4. A cautious implication or contribution.

Flag:

- missing design information
- vague findings
- causal overclaiming
- sentences that sound like filler
- claims not supported elsewhere in the paper

Return concise revision priorities, not a rewritten abstract unless asked.
