# Causal Language Audit

Audit whether the prose matches what the design and estimands support.

## Allowed language

Prefer:

- "is associated with" when describing non-experimental subgroup differences
- "respondents rate profiles differently" for conjoint outcomes
- "the AI frame shifts support relative to the offshoring frame" only when the treatment contrast is actually estimated
- "marginal means" or "AMCEs" when those are the estimands

## Flag language

Flag:

- broad claims about real-world labor-market behavior
- claims that AI or offshoring "causes" attitudes outside the experiment
- claims about voters, workers, or publics not represented by the sample
- mechanism claims not measured in the extension
- "proves", "demonstrates", "shows that" when the result is tentative or null

Return exact phrases to revise and a safer replacement.
